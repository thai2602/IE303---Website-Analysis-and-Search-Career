### 4.2 Content Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's a Problem | Fix |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| Generic job titles | Doesn't match JD role labels | Mirror the exact job title from the JD |
| Vague skill descriptions | No keyword match | Use specific tool/skill names |
| Missing dates | ATS can't calculate experience duration | Always include MM/YYYY for all roles |
| Unexplained gaps | May trigger low scoring | Add a brief note (e.g. "Career break — professional development") |
| Submitting one CV for all roles | Low keyword match per JD | Tailor each CV to the specific JD |
