# CV Scoring Framework (Scorecard) for HR Professionals

## Metadata
- **Topic**: CV / Resume Scoring and Shortlisting
- **Audience**: HR Professionals, Recruiters, Hiring Managers
- **Language**: English

---

## 1. Purpose & Value of a CV Scorecard

A CV Scorecard acts as a structured guide that helps HR clearly identify high-potential candidates from those who should be screened out. When properly designed and applied, it delivers the following benefits:

- **Standardizes the selection process** across HR team members, ensuring consistent decisions
- **Saves screening time** by replacing gut-feel judgments with a clear scoring system
- **Enables holistic candidate evaluation** — covering hard skills, soft skills, and cultural fit
- **Reduces mis-hire risk** and supports data-driven decisions over subjective impressions

> A well-built CV Scorecard helps junior HR professionals screen confidently and raises the overall professionalism of the recruitment function.

## 2. CV Scorecard Template

### Scoring Criteria

| # | Criterion | Description | Max Score | Scoring Guide |
|---|-----------|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| 1 | **Professional Qualifications & Education** | Evaluate degree, certifications, and skills directly relevant to the role | 20 | **20 pts**: Correct field of study, relevant certifications. **10 pts**: Related background but requires additional training. |
| 2 | **Work Experience** | Assess years of experience, industry, and similarity to the JD | 25 | **25 pts**: 2+ years in an equivalent role. **15 pts**: Similar experience but at a lower level. |
| 3 | **Notable Achievements & Projects** | Based on concrete results, specific projects, or quantified evidence | 20 | **20 pts**: Clear achievements backed by data/metrics. **10 pts**: General descriptions without specific evidence. |
| 4 | **Soft Skills & Work Attitude** | Evaluate communication, collaboration, critical thinking, and learning agility | 15 | **15 pts**: Skills demonstrated through real examples. **10 pts**: Skills listed only, without supporting evidence. |
| 5 | **Cultural Fit** | Assess whether personal values, attitude, and career goals align with the company | 10 | **10 pts**: Strong alignment with company values and environment. **5 pts**: Likely to adapt within a short period. |
| 6 | **CV Presentation & Reliability** | Evaluate layout, spelling, logical flow, and information credibility | 10 | **10 pts**: Clear, professional, well-structured CV. **5 pts**: Minor errors or inconsistencies present. |

**Total: 100 points**

---

## 3. Score Interpretation & Action Guide

| Score Range | Interpretation | Recommended Action |
|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| 80 – 100 | Strong candidate | Advance to interview round |
| 60 – 79 | Potential candidate | Hold for further review or preliminary interview |
| Below 60 | Does not meet requirements | Archive for future or alternative roles |

---

## 4. Best Practices for Applying the CV Scorecard

When rolling out a CV scoring system, HR should follow these key principles to ensure accuracy and effectiveness:

- **Customize per role**: Avoid using a single fixed template across all JDs — adjust criteria weighting based on role priorities
- **Maintain consistency across reviewers**: Especially when multiple HR members are evaluating the same batch of CVs
- **Integrate with ATS or AI tools**: Automate keyword filtering and preliminary scoring where possible
- **Add qualitative notes alongside scores**: Written comments provide deeper context during internal review discussions
